Smart Symlink
How it works

Your drive is full. The folders can't move. Now they can.

Games, virtual machines, caches and downloads sit exactly where programs expect them. Smart Symlink moves the data and keeps the old path alive.

  1. 1

    Pick what takes up space

    The left pane shows your folders with their real sizes. Select one or several.

  2. 2

    Pick where it should live

    Open a folder on another drive in the right pane, then drag the selection over or press Link.

  3. 3

    Done, and nothing breaks

    The data is moved and checked. A link at the old path sends every program to the new place.

Features

Everything for links, nothing extra

Built for one job and made to be safe at it.

Two panes

Source on the left, destination on the right, like an FTP client. Both start at your drives with size and free space.

Safe by design

Copy, check, then switch. If a step fails, it is undone and your folder stays as it was.

The right link type

Junction, symbolic link, hard link, clone and more. Each one is explained, and the best one is preselected.

Folder sizes

See which folders are worth moving. Sizes are calculated in the background and you can sort by them.

Unlink any time

Changed your mind? Unlink moves the data back to its old place, or just removes the link.

Mouse, touch or keyboard

Drag and drop, tap, or never leave the keyboard: arrow keys or h j k l, and Ctrl+Enter to link.

A closer look

Clear before, clear after

Start

All drives at a glance

Both panes open on your computer: every drive with its size and free space, plus Quick Access for folders you pin with a star.

Confirm

You see what will happen

Before anything moves, the dialog lists each folder, where its data goes and what kind of link takes its place.

Links

Follow a link to its target

Links have their own colors. Select one and the right pane shows the folder it points to. One press on Unlink brings the data back. Shown here in the light theme.

History

Every link you made, in one list

Saved links remembers what you moved and where. It also tells you when a target has gone missing, for example an unplugged drive.

Careful with your data

Nothing about your files changes

A moved folder is the same folder in a new place. Every item is read back and compared after copying. Any difference stops the move before the original is touched.
